How Often Should You Flush a Water Heater?

By Jane Doe, Master Plumber. March 1, 2025

Flush a tank water heater once a year. Hard water areas need it every six months. Sediment builds up at the bottom of the tank. It makes the heater work harder and wear out sooner. Our water heater service covers this.

What are the signs it needs flushing now?

Popping or rumbling sounds are the first sign. Rusty water and slow hot water are others. The Department of Energy explains how sediment cuts efficiency. See also tankless vs tank and when to replace the anode rod.

How long does it take?

Most flushes take under an hour. You need a garden hose, a bucket and a screwdriver. Turn off the power or gas first. Then open the drain valve and let the water run clear. Close the valve and refill the tank before you turn the heat back on. This keeps the heating element safe. It also stops air from getting trapped in the lines. Check for leaks at the valve when you are done. If the valve drips, tighten it a little. If it still drips, call a plumber. Some valves are plastic and break with age. A brass valve lasts much longer and costs a few dollars. We fit one on every heater we service. Ask us about it when you book.
